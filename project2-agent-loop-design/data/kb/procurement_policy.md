# Procurement and Renewal Policy

Hardware warranty renewals above 5,000 USD require Head of IT approval and two vendor quotes. Renewals at or below 5,000 USD can be approved by the infrastructure lead alone.

Warranty renewals must be initiated at least 45 days before expiry. Servers hosting identity services (domain controllers, IAM) are renewed at the 90-day mark regardless of cost.

End-of-life policy: laptops are refreshed on a 4-year cycle; servers on a 5-year cycle. Devices past end-of-life are not renewed - they are scheduled for replacement in the next procurement window.
